Our story · Our purpose

Meet BUBUNYOG.

Inspired by the thriving bee industry of Batangas, BUBUNYOG is a student-led sustainability initiative exploring how local resources, research, and creativity can come together to develop practical ideas for a more sustainable future.

Where it began

From a research project to a community initiative.

BUBUNYOG began with our research study, Practical Usability and Consumer Acceptability of Locally Formulated Beeswax Food Wrap. While exploring possible alternatives to disposable food coverings, we focused on beeswax because of its connection to the bee industry thriving in Batangas.

The beautiful apiaries and the people behind local bee products inspired us to explore a different use for beeswax. We wanted to investigate whether a food wrap formulated with unrefined beeswax and virgin coconut oil could be practical and acceptable to potential users.

Our research evaluates flexibility, adhesion and sealability, ease of use and handling, and respondents' willingness to adopt the product for personal or commercial use. BUBUNYOG is our proposed output: a platform to document our process, share our findings, develop a product guide, and introduce people to local bee resources.

Our direction

What we stand for

Our Mission

To investigate the practical usability and consumer acceptability of locally formulated beeswax food wrap and transform our findings into an accessible product guide. Through student-led research, clear documentation, and community awareness, we aim to encourage informed exploration of reusable food-storage alternatives and responsible appreciation of local bee resources.

Our Vision

A future where young people use research and creativity to explore sustainable solutions, communities value their local natural resources, and promising ideas are evaluated honestly before they are recommended for wider use.

What we are working toward

Our Goals

✦

Evaluate the wrap's flexibility, adhesion, sealability, and ease of use and handling.

✦

Understand students' willingness to adopt the wrap for personal and commercial use.

✦

Use research results and feedback to identify areas for improvement.

✦

Develop a product guide containing tested ingredients, quantities, preparation steps, photos, and a video demonstration.

✦

Highlight Batangas apiaries and explore responsible sourcing of locally available beeswax.

✦

Share what we learn about reusable food coverings, including their limitations and areas requiring further testing.
