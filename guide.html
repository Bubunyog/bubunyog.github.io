Safety and responsible use

The guide will include adult supervision and safe handling of hot wax. Beeswax wraps should not be used in ovens or microwaves and are generally unsuitable for direct contact with raw meat or other high-risk foods. Final food-use recommendations will be based on appropriate guidance and the limits of our testing.
