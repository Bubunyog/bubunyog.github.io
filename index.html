🐝 A STUDENT-LED SUSTAINABILITY PROJECT

Meet BUBUNYOG.

Small wraps. Big possibilities.

Inspired by Batangas' thriving bee industry, we're exploring locally formulated beeswax food wraps as a reusable alternative to disposable food coverings. Our student research puts usability, consumer feedback, and local resources at the heart of the process.
