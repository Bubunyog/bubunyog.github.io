The Sustainable Blend

Applying Our Usability Research

Explore the basic process behind a reusable beeswax wrap. Coconut oil is included in this project as a variable to investigate for flexibility and handling—not a guaranteed result. Record your observations and follow appropriate safety guidance.

Safety first

Use adult supervision and low heat. Avoid open flames, overheating wax, and direct contact with hot materials. Keep wraps away from raw meat, high heat, ovens, and microwaves.

This is an educational guide. Test recipes and food-contact safety carefully before using wraps with food.

Step 01

Prepare your materials

Gather clean, tightly woven cotton fabric, beeswax suitable for crafting, a small amount of coconut oil for the test blend, baking paper, a brush, and a heat-safe work surface. Cut fabric to your chosen size and record the materials used.

Research note: Keep one plain beeswax wrap as a comparison sample if your study design allows it.

Step 02

Prepare the blend

With adult supervision, gently melt the beeswax using a controlled, indirect-heat method. If testing coconut oil, measure and add only the amount specified in your approved experiment. Stir carefully and never leave heated wax unattended.

Research note: Record the exact proportions. Too much oil may affect the wrap’s finish or performance.

Step 03

Coat and cool the fabric

Place the fabric between sheets of baking paper on a protected, heat-safe surface. Apply a thin, even layer of the blend using an appropriate supervised method, following your project’s safety procedure. Allow the fabric to cool fully before handling.

Research note: Look for even coating and note any stiff, oily, or uneven areas.

Step 04

Test, compare, and reflect

After cooling, assess flexibility, grip, ease of folding, and how well the wrap conforms to a clean container. Compare samples consistently and gather feedback from participants using the same questions and conditions.

Research note: Report actual observations; do not claim that a blend is safer, more effective, or more sustainable without evidence.

Evidence over assumptions

Research Highlights

Why explore DIY wraps?

Reusable food wraps may offer a practical way to reduce reliance on some single-use coverings. Our usability research focuses on how easy they are to make, handle, clean, and reuse—and whether students consider them worthwhile over time.

Share your perspective

Ease of Use

Can students shape the wrap easily around containers? Does the material feel flexible, manageable, and convenient for everyday routines?

Long-Term Value

Could repeated use offset the initial cost? Compare material costs, expected lifespan, care needs, and the number of single-use wraps replaced.

What we want to learn

Student feedback, observed handling, durability, and transparent cost comparisons can help the team understand the strengths and limitations of DIY wraps. Findings should reflect the actual data collected through the project.

Meet the initiative

About BUBUNYOG

BUBUNYOG is a student-led sustainability research initiative that explores the usability of beeswax and coconut-based food wraps as a practical alternative to some single-use plastics. Through research, hands-on learning, and community participation, we aim to make sustainable habits feel more achievable for young people.
